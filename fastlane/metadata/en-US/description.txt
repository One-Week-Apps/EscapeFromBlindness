Escape From Blindness is an escape game you play entirely by ear, with VoiceOver, the iPhone's built-in screen reader.

Jack the explorer is trapped deep in a cavern with no torch left. The screen stays black: your ears guide you. Listen to the descriptions, explore each area with your finger, find the codes and solve the riddles to help him get out.

WHO IS IT FOR?
• Visually impaired players who use VoiceOver every day.
• Sighted players curious to discover how people use an iPhone without seeing it. A real challenge!

THE GAME
• 4 chapters and 12 levels: the Ice Cave, the Lost City, the Hidden Valley and the Hidden Tomb.
• Choices to make, door codes to find, passwords to remember and rooms to search.
• Hints when you are stuck: the first one is available right away, and the game tells you when the next one comes.
• Your progress is saved, and you can replay from chapter 1 without losing it.
• Sound effects and music, even in silent mode.
• In English and French.

VoiceOver is required to play: no cheating! The game shows you how to turn it on with a triple-click.
